# Code of Conduct

Skynet Datagrid Labs is a collaborative learning and engineering community. Members, contributors, reviewers, and participants are expected to help keep its repositories and discussions welcoming, respectful, and focused on the work.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect and discuss ideas without personal attacks.
- Give constructive, specific feedback and accept that contributors have different experience levels.
- Credit other people's work and respect licenses, privacy, and data-use restrictions.
- Keep discussion relevant, accessible, and considerate of different backgrounds.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention without consent, deliberate disruption, retaliation, and repeated abusive or degrading conduct are not acceptable. Publishing another person's private information or credentials is prohibited.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in organization repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and project-related community spaces. Organization maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or refer serious concerns to the organization owners. Actions should be proportionate to the conduct and consistently applied.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns, contact an organization owner privately using the contact information on the [organization profile](https://github.com/skynet-datagrid-labs). Do not include public accusations or unrelated personal information. For security vulnerabilities, use the repository's private **Report a vulnerability** flow instead. Reports will be handled as discreetly as practical, and retaliation for good-faith reporting is prohibited.
